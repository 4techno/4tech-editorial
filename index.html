How We Work

Clear thinking. Reviewable progress.

A practical process keeps the scope understandable and the engineering grounded.

01

Define the question

Clarify the engineering problem, physical constraints, and intended outcome before selecting components or promising a delivery date.

02

Make progress visible

Break the work into reviewable stages, document trade-offs, and distinguish a conceptual CAD model from a tested hardware subsystem.

03

Leave understanding behind

Explain the design rationale, share transparent schematics and firmware repositories, and make the next iteration step immediately obvious.
